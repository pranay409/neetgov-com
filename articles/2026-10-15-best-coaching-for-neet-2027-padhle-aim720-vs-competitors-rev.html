What Makes Good NEET Coaching?

Before we compare specific coaching centers, let's talk about what actually works. Good NEET coaching needs to have several things in place. First, you need live classes where you can ask doubts in real-time. Pre-recorded videos are convenient, but they won't help when you're confused about a concept at 10 PM. Second, you need a mentor who actually knows your weak areas and can guide you personally. Third, you need access to quality practice problems and previous year question papers. And finally, you need flexibility because life happens, and sometimes you miss a class.
