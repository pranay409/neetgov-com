Top Government Medical Colleges in India with NEET Cutoff Ranks

Published on 2026-08-31 | NEETGov Team

Getting into a government medical college through NEET is the dream of lakhs of students every year. But here's the good news: with the right preparation and understanding of cutoff trends, it's absolutely achievable. In this guide, we'll walk you through the top government medical colleges in India and their NEET cutoff ranks so you know exactly what score you need to aim for.

Understanding NEET Cutoff Ranks

Before we jump into college lists, let's clarify what cutoff ranks actually mean. The cutoff rank is the last rank that gets admission into a particular college for a particular category. If your rank is better than the cutoff, you're in. If not, you need to try again next year or consider other colleges.

These cutoffs vary every year based on several factors: the number of candidates, difficulty of the exam, number of available seats, and how many students from your category are competing. So when we share 2024 or 2025 cutoff data, remember that 2026 cutoffs will be different. Use these as reference points, not absolute numbers.

AIIMS Colleges: The Most Prestigious Options

Let's start with the colleges that top students dream about. AIIMS Delhi is typically the hardest to crack, with general category cutoff ranks usually in the range of 1 to 100. AIIMS Delhi consistently sees the best all-India rank candidates competing for its seats.

Other top AIIMS colleges include AIIMS Mumbai, AIIMS Bangalore, and AIIMS Hyderabad. These typically have cutoff ranks between 100 and 500 for general category. If you're an OBC or SC/ST candidate, your cutoff rank would be higher (meaning easier to get in) because of the separate merit lists maintained.

The thing about AIIMS is that they're all-India quota colleges, meaning you compete with students from across the country. The entrance is competitive, but the faculty, infrastructure, and placements make them worth every bit of effort.

Top Government Medical Colleges by State

Delhi Region

Besides AIIMS Delhi, colleges like Maulana Azad Medical College and Delhi Medical College are excellent options. Cutoff ranks for these usually fall between 200 and 1000 for general category, depending on the year.

Maharashtra

BJ Medical College in Pune and Grant Medical College in Mumbai are highly sought after. General category cutoffs typically range from 300 to 1200.

Karnataka

Bangalore Medical College and Mysore Medical College offer good seats. Cutoff ranks are usually between 500 and 2000 for general category.

Tamil Nadu

Government medical colleges in Tamil Nadu like Madras Medical College and Stanley Medical College have more seats available, so cutoffs tend to be higher (around 3000 to 8000) but still very much achievable with solid preparation.

State Medical Colleges: Your Real Gateway

Here's what many students don't realize: most government medical seats are in state colleges, not AIIMS. This is actually your real opportunity. States like UP, MP, Gujarat, and Rajasthan have numerous government medical colleges with decent cutoff ranks.

For example, King George's Medical University in Lucknow often has cutoff ranks between 5000 and 15000. Banaras Hindu University in Varanasi might see cutoffs around 2000 to 5000. These are still competitive, but much more achievable than AIIMS if you're putting in consistent effort.

The trick is researching which state you want to study in and understanding that particular state's cutoff trends. Each state conducts its own counseling process alongside the all-India quota.

Practical Strategy: Know Your Target Range

Here's what you should do right now. Check the official NEET counseling website and download the previous year's cutoff ranks for colleges in your preferred state. Make a list of colleges where you think your rank might fall. Then work backwards: what score do you need to achieve that rank?

For instance, if you live in Gujarat and want to study there, look at all government medical colleges in Gujarat, check their 2024 cutoffs, and see where you'd fit. This gives you a much clearer picture than just thinking about AIIMS.

The Role of Category Reservations

If you're an OBC, SC, or ST candidate, your cutoff rank for the same college will be higher (easier to get in). Don't get discouraged if the general category rank seems out of reach. Check the reserved category cutoffs separately. Many excellent candidates from reserved categories get into top colleges because of this system.

Closing the Gap: Quality Preparation is Key

Understanding cutoff ranks is half the battle. The other half is actually preparing well enough to achieve the rank you're targeting. This requires structured coaching, consistent practice, and regular mock tests.
